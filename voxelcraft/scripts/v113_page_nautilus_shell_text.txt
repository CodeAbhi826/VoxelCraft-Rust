 A nautilus shell is an item that is rarely dropped by killed nautiluses , always dropped by drowned that hold them, and it can be fished out of water . Nautilus shells, along with hearts of the sea , are required to craft conduits .

 Obtaining [ edit | edit source ] 
 Mob loot [ edit | edit source ] 
 Drowned have a 3%‌ [ JE only ] or 8%‌ [ BE only ] chance to spawn with a nautilus shell in their off-hand, and they always drop that nautilus shell upon death . In Bedrock Edition , this also includes zombies converted into drowned.
 In Bedrock Edition , wandering traders have a chance of 25% (increased by 1% per level of Looting ) to drop 1 nautilus shell when killed while showing the item as trade offer, if the player holds an emerald .

 Fishing [ edit | edit source ] 
 Main article: Fishing § Items obtainable 
 Nautilus shells are obtainable through fishing as a treasure item, with a 0.8% chance to obtain one, per catch, without Luck of the Sea . Like other treasure items, this chance is then increased to 1.2% with Luck of the Sea I, 1.5% with Luck of the Sea II, and 1.9% with Luck of the Sea III.
 Trading [ edit | edit source ] 
 Wandering traders may sell 1 nautilus shell for 5 emeralds .

 Trivia [ edit | edit source ]
